Common Questions

To get started, click the 'Register' button in the top navigation bar. Fill in your personal information and details about your technical diploma. Once you submit the form, verify your email address. After logging in, you can immediately check your eligibility status and access the dashboard.

Go to the 'Check Eligibility' page from the dashboard or main menu. Select your diploma type, year of graduation, and specialization. The system will automatically calculate your eligibility percentage based on the Ministry of Higher Education's latest equivalency rules.

You generally need: A copy of your high school certificate, a copy of your technical diploma, the 'Nata'ij Al-Tahseel' (Grades Sheet), birth certificate, national ID card, and 6 recent personal photos. Some faculties may require additional specific documents.
